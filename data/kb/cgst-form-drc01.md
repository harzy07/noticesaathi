### Summary of Notice Fields
1. Brief facts of the case: Summary of audit, inspection, scrutiny, or intelligence findings.
2. Grounds: Legal provisions alleged to have been violated (e.g. Section 16, Section 29, Section 39).
3. Tax and other dues:
Table specifying Tax (CGST, SGST, IGST, Cess), Interest (calculated under section 50), and Penalty (proposed under relevant sections).
4. Opportunity of Hearing:
You are hereby required to file your reply in FORM GST DRC-06 within thirty days from the service of this notice and appear for a personal hearing on the scheduled date and venue if an adverse decision is contemplated.
