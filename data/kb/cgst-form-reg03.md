### Form GST REG-03 Notice Structure

### Notice Queries & Demands
Subject: Notice for seeking additional information / clarification / documents relating to application for registration.

1. This has reference to your application for registration filed vide ARN [ARN] dated [Date].
2. On examination of your application and supporting documents, the following queries / deficiencies have been noticed:
- Proof of principal place of business (electricity bill, rent agreement, NOC).
- Identity / address proof of authorized signatory, partners, or directors.
- Clarification on trade name, constitution, or jurisdiction.
3. You are required to submit clarification or documents in FORM GST REG-04 within seven working days from the date of receipt of this notice. If no clarification is received within the specified period, your application is liable to be rejected without further notice.
