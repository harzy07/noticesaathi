### Non-Statutory Guidance Notice
*Notice: This explainer is practical informational guidance for taxpayers and accountants and does not constitute statutory text or official legal advice.*

### Key Points for Notice in Form GSTR-3A
1. Purpose: Issued under Section 46 and Rule 68 when a registered taxpayer fails to furnish periodic returns (GSTR-3B, GSTR-1, GSTR-4, or GSTR-9) by the statutory due date.
2. Compliance Window: 15 days from the date of the notice.
3. Required Remedy: Unlike ASMT-10 or DRC-01 where a letter is drafted, the mandatory legal remedy for GSTR-3A is to file the overdue GST return along with applicable late fees under Section 47 and interest under Section 50.
4. Consequence of Failure: If returns are not filed within 15 days, the proper officer can proceed to Best Judgment Assessment under Section 62, estimating liability and issuing an ex-parte demand in Form GST ASMT-13.
