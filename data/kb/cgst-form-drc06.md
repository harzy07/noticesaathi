### Structure of Submissions
1. Preliminary Objections: Jurisdiction, limitation period under section 73(10)/74(10)/74A(2), validity of DIN, and statutory compliance.
2. Reply on Merits: Point-wise rebuttals to allegations raised in SCN, substantiated by invoice listings, bank statements, ledger accounts, and e-way bills.
3. Statutory Grounds & Precedents: Citations to relevant sections, rules, notifications, circulars, and judicial decisions.
4. Personal Hearing Request: Request for grant of personal hearing under section 75(4) of the CGST Act before passing any adverse adjudication order.
5. List of Enclosures: Annexures and documentary evidence relied upon.
