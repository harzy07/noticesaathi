### Cause of Payment
1. Voluntary payment under section 73(5) / 74(5) / 74A(5).
2. Payment against intimation in Form GST DRC-01A.
3. Payment against show cause notice in Form GST DRC-01 within thirty days under section 73(8) / 74(8) / 74A(8).
4. Payment against audit findings, inspection, or annual return reconciliation.

Payment Particulars:
- Tax head breakdown (CGST, SGST, IGST, Cess).
- Interest paid under section 50.
- Penalty paid (if applicable).
- Electronic Cash Ledger debit reference number and date.
