### Notice Grounds
Subject: Show Cause Notice for Cancellation of Registration under section 29 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017.

Whereas on the basis of information available in this office, you have:
1. Failed to furnish returns for a continuous period of six months (or three consecutive tax periods for quarterly filers); OR
2. Conducted business from a place other than the declared principal place of business; OR
3. Obtained registration by means of fraud, wilful misstatement or suppression of facts; OR
4. Issued invoices without supply of underlying goods or services in violation of Act.

You are hereby called upon to show cause within a period of seven working days from the date of service of this notice as to why your registration should not be cancelled. You are directed to file your reply in FORM GST REG-18 and appear for a personal hearing before the undersigned.
