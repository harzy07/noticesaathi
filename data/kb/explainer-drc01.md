### Non-Statutory Guidance Notice
*Notice: This explainer is practical informational guidance for businesses and chartered accountants and does not constitute statutory text or official legal advice.*

### Key Points for Form GST DRC-01
1. Nature of the Notice: Form GST DRC-01 is a formal Show Cause Notice proposing adjudication of tax liability, mandatory interest under Section 50, and penalties under Section 73, 74, or 74A.
2. Statutory Timeframe: The taxpayer has 30 days from the date of service to file a point-wise response in Form GST DRC-06.
3. Amnesty on Early Payment: In non-fraud matters under Section 73, if tax and interest are paid within 30 days of the SCN, penalty is entirely waived and proceedings conclude. Under Section 74 (fraud), penalty is reduced to 25% if paid within 30 days. Under Section 74A (FY 2024-25 onwards), penalty is completely waived for timely payment within 30 days in non-fraud cases.
4. Crucial Safeguard: Always demand an opportunity for Personal Hearing under Section 75(4) before any adverse order is confirmed.
