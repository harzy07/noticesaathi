### Rule 100(1)
The order of assessment made under sub-section (1) of section 62 shall be issued in FORM GST ASMT-13 and a summary thereof shall be uploaded electronically in FORM GST DRC-07.

### Rule 100(2)
The proper officer shall issue a notice to a taxable person in accordance with the provisions of section 63 in FORM GST ASMT-14 containing the grounds on which the assessment is proposed to be made on best judgment basis and after considering the representation, if any, made by such person, pass an order in FORM GST ASMT-15 and a summary thereof shall be uploaded electronically in FORM GST DRC-07.
