### Non-Statutory Guidance Notice
*Notice: This explainer is practical informational guidance for businesses and compliance professionals and does not constitute statutory text or official legal advice.*

### Key Points for Form GST ASMT-10
1. Nature of the Notice: Form GST ASMT-10 is an exploratory discrepancy intimation issued under Section 61 and Rule 99. It is NOT an assessment order or a final tax demand.
2. Common Causes: Typical discrepancies arise from differences between outward supplies declared in GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B, or differences in Input Tax Credit (ITC) between GSTR-2A/2B and GSTR-3B.
3. Response Form and Deadline: The taxpayer must reply within 30 calendar days from the date of service using Form GST ASMT-11 on the GST Portal.
4. Consequences of Inaction: If no reply is filed or if the explanation is unsatisfactory, the officer can escalate by initiating audit (Section 65) or issuing a formal Show Cause Notice (Section 73, 74, or 74A).
