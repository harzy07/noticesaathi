### Notice Text & Structure
Subject: Notice for intimating discrepancies in the return after scrutiny under section 61 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017.

1. This is to inform you that upon scrutiny of the return furnished by you under section 39 for the tax period referred to above, the discrepancies mentioned in the table below have been noticed.
2. You are hereby called upon to furnish an explanation for the said discrepancies within a period of thirty days of the service of this notice, or such further period as may be permitted, in FORM GST ASMT-11.
3. If no explanation is received within the said period, or if the explanation furnished is not found acceptable, further proceedings will be initiated without any further notice in accordance with the provisions of section 65, 66, 67 or section 73 or 74 or 74A of the Act.
