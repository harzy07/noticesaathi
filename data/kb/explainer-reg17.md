### Non-Statutory Guidance Notice
*Notice: This explainer is practical informational guidance for taxpayers and CAs and does not constitute statutory text or official legal advice.*

### Key Points for Form GST REG-17
1. Severity Level: CRITICAL. This is a show cause notice proposing cancellation of the taxpayer's GSTIN under Section 29(2) and Rule 22(1).
2. Strict Tolling: Reply must be submitted in Form GST REG-18 within 7 WORKING DAYS from the date of service.
3. Common Grounds: Continuous non-filing of returns for 6 months (or 3 quarters for QRMP filers), premises found locked during physical verification, or suspected bill trading without underlying supplies.
4. Immediate Action Required: File all pending returns with late fees immediately, gather physical evidence of genuine business at the registered address (geotagged photos, electricity bills, lease), and request revocation of suspension.
