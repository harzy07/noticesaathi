### Non-Statutory Guidance Notice
*Notice: This explainer is practical informational guidance for businesses and does not constitute statutory text or official legal advice.*

### Key Points for Form GST REG-03
1. Purpose: Issued under Rule 9(2) when the registration officer seeks additional documents or clarifications on a new GST application.
2. Deadline Rule: The taxpayer must file clarification in Form GST REG-04 within exactly 7 WORKING DAYS (skipping Saturdays, Sundays, and gazetted holidays) from the date of receipt.
3. Common Reasons: Discrepant electricity bill, lack of owner NOC, name mismatch with PAN database, or non-upload of authorized signatory resolution.
4. Consequence of Missing Deadline: Automatic rejection of the application without further communication.
