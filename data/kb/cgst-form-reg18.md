### Form GST REG-18 Reply Format
GSTIN: [Taxpayer GSTIN]
Legal Name: [Taxpayer Legal Name]
Reference No. of Notice: [REG-17 Reference Number]
Date of Notice: [Notice Date]

### Submissions & Grounds
Subject: Reply to show cause notice for cancellation of registration.

1. Factual Submissions:
Explanation of why the grounds alleged in REG-17 are unfounded or have been rectified.
Where non-filing of returns was alleged, details of all pending returns furnished and associated taxes, interest, and late fees paid.
Where place of business verification was disputed, proof of genuine business activity, electricity bills, GST signboard photos, and geo-tagged site images.
2. Hardship and Legal Precedents:
Cancellation of registration causes irreversible business disruption, deprivation of livelihood, and prevents compliance.
3. Prayer:
Request to drop the proposed cancellation proceedings and issue an order in FORM GST REG-20 revoking suspension and restoring registration.
