### Non-Statutory Guidance Notice
*Notice: This explainer is practical informational guidance for taxpayers and consultants and does not constitute statutory text or official legal advice.*

### Key Points for Form GST DRC-01A
1. Nature of the Intimation: Form GST DRC-01A is an advance intimation of tax liability ascertained by the proper officer before issuing a formal Show Cause Notice, issued under Rule 142(1A).
2. Objective: To promote voluntary dispute resolution, avoid protracted litigation, and give the taxpayer a chance to explain or settle dues without high penalty exposure.
3. Response Options:
- Agree and Pay: Make voluntary payment using Form GST DRC-03.
- Disagree or Partial Disagreement: File detailed factual explanations and rebuttals in Part B of Form GST DRC-01A.
4. Window: No rigid statutory days window is mandated by statute; check the specific date mentioned on the face of the notice.
