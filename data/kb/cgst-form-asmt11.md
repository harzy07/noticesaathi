### Reply Content and Format
Subject: Reply to notice issued under section 61 intimating discrepancies in return.

1. Explanation of Discrepancies:
The taxpayer provides point-wise reconciliation, documentary explanation, and legal justification for each discrepancy highlighted in FORM GST ASMT-10.
2. Acceptance and Voluntary Payment (if applicable):
Details of discrepancy accepted, tax/interest paid via electronic cash ledger, and payment challan reference numbers (FORM GST DRC-03).
3. Non-Acceptance of Discrepancy:
Factual clarification, reconciliation with GSTR-1 / GSTR-2B / GSTR-3B, supplier confirmation certificates, and supporting commercial documents.
4. Prayer:
Request to the proper officer to accept the explanation, conclude proceedings under section 61(2), and issue an order of acceptance in FORM GST ASMT-12.
