### Form GST REG-04 Submission Format
Reference No. of Notice: [REG-03 Reference Number]
Date of Notice: [Notice Date]
Application Reference Number (ARN): [ARN]
Name of the Applicant: [Applicant Name]

### Clarification Details
1. Response to Queries Raised in REG-03: Point-wise clarification addressing each specific query raised by the proper officer regarding business premises, legal status, or documentation.
2. List of Documents Uploaded:
- Validated rent deed, lease agreement, or property tax receipt.
- No Objection Certificate (NOC) from property owner.
- Identity and address verification documents.
- Bank statement or cancelled cheque.
3. Prayer: Request to accept clarification and approve grant of GST registration.
